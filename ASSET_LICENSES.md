# Asset license register

Every bundled visual, sound, font, texture, icon, illustration and sample asset must be registered here before release.

For each asset record:

- repository path or stable built-in asset ID;
- source/creator;
- license and its SPDX identifier where available;
- attribution text and required attribution location;
- modifications made by FigmentFell;
- whether redistribution inside `.ffstory`, `.fftheme` or `.ffdeck` is permitted.

## Current assets

No third-party visual or audio assets are bundled yet. `src/assets/react.svg` is the Vite starter asset and is not product artwork; it must be removed or replaced before the first product release.
